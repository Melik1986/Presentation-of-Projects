We invest time, attention, and context into our work.

But typical freelance platforms and portfolio services often erase individuality —

## What is Starlight?

Starlight isn't just a template.
It's a presentation structure where each project gets its own space, accents, and rhythm.

- **Folder catalogs** gently guide the viewer
- **Sticky cards** create an immersive effect
- **Audio** supports the scene's mood
- **Content** edited through simple JSON files
- **Easy deployment** on Vercel

The goal of Starlight is to give creative people a place where work "resonates".
